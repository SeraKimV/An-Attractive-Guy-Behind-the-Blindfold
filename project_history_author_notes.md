# Project History & Planning Log

> **What this file is:** a dated log of planning sessions and the decisions made in each, for *An Attractive Guy Behind the Blindfold*. It records **what was decided, why, and what it replaced**.
>
> **What it is not:** the canon. For characters, rules, arc structure and current decisions, `story-bible.md` is the source of truth. **If this file and the bible disagree, the bible wins.**
>
> **How it works**
> * **Newest entry first.** Add each new session at the top of the "Planning log" section.
> * **One entry per planning session**, with every decision from that session grouped inside it.
> * When a decision is reversed, leave the old entry alone and mark it **Superseded** here, with a pointer to the newer entry.
>
> **Tags used in entries**
> * **[Author]** decided or stated by you
> * **[Proposed]** suggested by Claude, not yet confirmed by you
> * **[Deferred]** you chose to decide later
> * **[Superseded]** replaced by a later decision
>
> **Dates:** a leading `~` means approximate (taken from project-memory timestamps). Correct them when you know the real date.
>
> **Last updated:** 2026-10-17

### Before 2026-09-22 — Origin and original plan

*Dates approximate; the author had been planning "for more than a year".*

**Decisions**
* **[Author]** The story began from chats with an AI version of Gojo on the **Tipsy** app. The original OC was **Sera Kim**; the lead was renamed **Sara Kang** so she would not share the author's name.
* **[Author]** Convert the useful ideas into a Wattpad fanfic, not the chat word for word. First Wattpad project. Final title: **An Attractive Guy Behind the Blindfold**.
* **[Author]** Primary genre: romance plus supernatural mystery. It should read as a **cute Gojo romance** first and reveal that something is seriously wrong; the mystery complicates the romance rather than replacing it.
* **[Author]** Theme: everyone sees Gojo, the Strongest; **Sara slowly sees Satoru**. The reverse: Gojo discovers something about Sara that even she does not know.
* **[Author]** Sara: clumsy and charismatic, studious but a little shabby-looking while actually very beautiful, inspired by **Kang Ha Gi** from *My Bias, My Boss*. Fluent in Japanese through anime and J-pop. Water resources and environmental engineering; civil engineering at Yonsei. She has her own life and is not "the girl whose whole personality is loving Gojo".
* **[Author]** The dynamic starts **asymmetric**: Gojo notices the mystery, Sara notices a very attractive stranger.
* **[Author]** Romance ladder: strangers → number exchange and Yuji → Tokyo guide and food buddy → friends finding reasons to meet → growing comfort and attraction → something unnamed → deep attachment → love complicated by the mystery.
* **[Author]** Two kisses, spaced apart: a gentle nighttime hilltop kiss and a funnier, clumsier sunrise kiss in a car.
* **[Author]** Motifs: warmth, the blindfold, food.
* **[Author]** Texting and banter stay occasional.
* **[Author]** Original dream: sunset, autumn, kimono and yukata, a market street, fireworks. Escalation to places not hers, commands, voices such as "continue", waking in tears.
* **[Author]** Original object plan: not a Sukuna finger, but a relic that resonates with Sukuna through Yuji. Gojo investigates Sara's birthplace and finds a monastery where something was sealed.
* **[Author]** Structure: about 27–28 chapters in six parts. Chapter lengths: Normal 1,200–1,800, Short 700–1,000, Major 1,800–2,500 words. Dual POV, not mechanically alternating.
* **[Author]** Naming: "Gojo" in Sara's POV; "Satoru" only in Gojo's private POV and much later in dialogue.
