# Project History & Author Notes

> **Purpose:** Historical/project-management notes for *An Attractive Guy Behind the Blindfold*.
>
> **Canon rule:** This file is **not** the story bible. For characters, supernatural rules, timeline, arc structure, naming conventions, and current canon, always use `story-bible(1).md` as the source of truth. Notes below preserve development history and author decisions without overriding the current bible.

## 1. Project Origin

- The story began from conversations with an AI version of Satoru Gojo on the Tipsy app.
- The original OC was named **Sera Kim**; the female lead was later renamed **Sara Kang** intentionally so the character would not share the author's name.
- The goal was to turn the useful ideas and dialogue beats from the Tipsy conversations into a Wattpad fanfiction rather than reproduce the chat word-for-word.
- The finalized title is **An Attractive Guy Behind the Blindfold**.
- This is the author's first Wattpad writing project.
- The author had read only a small number of Wattpad stories while starting the project and had watched *Jujutsu Kaisen* Season 1.

## 2. Early Story Development

The initial concept was a Gojo x Sara romance that would gradually reveal a supernatural mystery.

Important development decisions from the early planning stage:

- The story should initially feel like a cute Gojo romance before readers realize that something is seriously wrong.
- The supernatural mystery should complicate the romance rather than replace it.
- Sara should have her own life, studies, personality, and opinions rather than existing only as Gojo's love interest.
- The initial dynamic was intentionally asymmetric: Gojo notices that something about Sara is unusual, while Sara mainly notices that Gojo is extremely attractive/cool.
- The original first-meeting material included Gojo's ridiculous self-introduction, his offer to show Sara around Tokyo, Sara needing to get to the library, and Sara unexpectedly asking for his number.
- The original "warmth" idea was retained, but the novel version was deliberately adjusted so it feels like something Sara says instinctively rather than as an overly deep analysis of a stranger.

## 3. Character Development Notes — Sara

Sara's characterization was refined during drafting.

- She developed into a **clumsy, charismatic, studious girl** who can look somewhat shabby/common while actually being very beautiful.
- The characterization was inspired in part by the author's reference to a K-drama character from *My Bias, My Boss*.
- Sara is fluent in Japanese because of her interest in anime and J-pop.
- Her academic background was refined from a generic engineering concept into **water resources/environmental engineering**, with a civil-engineering undergraduate background at Yonsei noted during development.
- A recurring practical/clumsy beat was established when Sara fumbles with her guesthouse keys.
- The author wanted Gojo/Sara texting and banter to remain occasional rather than becoming their main form of communication.

## 4. Character Development Notes — Gojo & Sara Romance

The intended relationship progression developed as:

**interesting strangers → number exchange + Yuji connection → Tokyo guide/food buddy → friends who keep finding reasons to spend time together → increasing emotional comfort and physical attraction → something neither wants to name → deep emotional attachment → love complicated by the supernatural mystery**

Other romance-development notes:

- Warmth, the blindfold, and food became recurring motifs.
- The blindfold began as Sara's visual curiosity about Gojo and later gained symbolic meaning connected to his public persona/emotional armor.
- Food scenes were intended to create ordinary, warm relationship moments rather than always serving the plot.
- Two future kiss ideas were discussed: a gentle nighttime hilltop kiss and a separate, funnier/clumsier sunrise kiss in a car. They were intended to be spaced apart rather than treated as consecutive beats.

## 5. Original Tipsy Material — Useful Beats

The Tipsy conversations served as raw material rather than fixed dialogue.

Useful beats retained during development included:

- Gojo is with Yuji, Megumi, and Nobara after a mission when he senses Sara.
- His cursed energy reacts to her and his blindfold lifts.
- He wonders who she is.
- He gives his exaggerated self-introduction: teacher, genius, strongest, handsome.
- He offers to show Sara around Tokyo.
- Sara says she needs the library.
- Sara unexpectedly asks for his number.
- Sara describes Gojo as reliable/warm despite barely knowing him.
- Later material included Tokyo exploration, food, texting, teasing, dreams, and increasing physical/emotional closeness.

These beats should continue to be treated as **development source material**, not mandatory line-for-line dialogue. The current story bible controls the final version.

## 6. Chapter Development History — Part I

### Chapter 1

- Title: **The Girl Who Made Him Look Twice**
- Chapter 1 was published on Wattpad before the later chapters.
- Early reader response included 8 views and 2 comments at the time of the author's update.
- One commenter asked about connecting on Discord.
- Another commenter, who described themselves as a paid comic artist, offered to adapt the story into a comic.

### Chapter 2

- Title: **The Big Guy Who Had Warmth**
- Yuji recognizes Sara near the library, revealing that Sara and Yuji already knew each other online before the story began.

### Chapter 3

- Title: **Ramen, Kikufuku, and a Chaperone**
- Yuji joins Gojo and Sara's first outing.
- Sara resolves a food-choice conflict.
- Kikufuku/matcha mochi is established as one of Gojo's favorite desserts.
- Sara fumbles with her guesthouse keys, reinforcing her clumsy-character trait.
- The chapter ends with Gojo and Sara texting to confirm a just-the-two-of-them outing for the following day (Saturday).

### Chapter 4

- Title: **Mars, Pancakes, and a Bad Sense of Balance**
- Sara's enrollment day.
- A stop for a "jiggly pancake" dessert inspired by a viral food video.
- Sara talks about her hometown south of Seoul and its temple/monastery-filled surroundings.
- The National Astronomical Observatory in Mitaka provides the setting for an observatory scene.
- Gojo catches Sara when she nearly falls on the stone steps.
- They view Mars and the Milky Way.
- Sara has to hurry back to her guesthouse before curfew.
- The chapter plants the blindfold as a visual curiosity hook for Sara.

### Part I Status

- **Part I — The Stranger (Chapters 1–4) is closed/finished.**
- The project then moved to Part II: **Somewhere Between Friends + 1st Dream**.

## 9. Development Planning Notes

Earlier planning discussed approximately **24–30 chapters** and a rough split between relationship-building and the supernatural/curse arc.

The project later became more structured into approximately **27–28 chapters across six parts**. The current story bible is authoritative for that structure, so the earlier 24–30 chapter estimate is retained here only as development history.

## 10. Superseded / Obsolete Notes

### Timeline — DO NOT USE AS CURRENT CANON

An older planning note placed the story in early/mid **2019**, after the Kyoto Goodwill Event.

This was later changed.

For current story continuity, use the updated story bible instead. The current canon establishes the story at the **beginning of September 2018**, before the Kyoto Goodwill Event, with the AU timeline also delaying the Shibuya Incident to 2021.

### Earlier Chapter-Length Planning

An older note used a general target of approximately 1200–1800 words per chapter.

The current story bible later refined this into Normal / Short / Major chapter bands. Use the story bible for current chapter-length guidance.

### Earlier Broad Curse-Arc Description

Earlier notes described Sara simply as an engineer/PhD student who would develop an ability after being born near a cursed Buddhist monastery, find a cursed object during excavation, hear voices, and become involved with Sukuna through Yuji.

The concept was subsequently refined in the story bible. The refined version is authoritative and should be used for current drafting.
