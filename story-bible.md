# Initial Idea

# An Attractive Guy Behind the Blindfold \- Initial Idea

# Genre/Tone

Primary:

* Romance  
* Supernatural mystery

Secondary:

* Slow burn  
* Fluff  
* Comedy  
* Emotional/angsty moments  
* Fantasy/supernatural  
* Eventually darker supernatural/psychological tension

The story should **feel romance-first initially**, with the supernatural mystery gradually becoming more prominent.

The goal is for readers to initially think:

> “Cute Gojo romance.”

Then gradually realize:

> “Wait... something is seriously wrong here.”

The supernatural mystery should **complicate the romance, not replace it**.

---

# Central Theme Idea

The main emotional theme developed for the story is:

> **Everyone sees Satoru Gojo, the Strongest. Sara slowly sees Satoru.**

Gojo is used to people seeing:

* the strongest sorcerer  
* the Six Eyes  
* someone terrifying/untouchable  
* an extremely powerful teacher  
* an attractive man  
* someone people fear or admire

Sara, however, instinctively experiences him differently.

She eventually tells him that she finds him **reliable** and that he gives her a strange sense of **warmth**, despite being a stranger.

This affects Gojo deeply because people don't normally describe him as warm or safe.

The reverse theme is also important:

Everyone assumes Sara is an ordinary Korean PhD student, but Gojo eventually discovers there is something unusual about her that **even Sara doesn't know about herself**.

# Sara Kang

Sara is a Korean international student in Tokyo.

She is doing a PhD in:

> **Environmental and Water Management Engineering**

Her engineering/research background is important to the actual plot and should not just be background decoration.

Eventually her research/site work leads her to an excavation/site where she encounters a cursed object.

### **Sara's personality**

She should be:

* intelligent  
* practical  
* independent  
* curious  
* occasionally shy when romance becomes direct  
* straightforward  
* capable of teasing Gojo  
* not obsessed with Gojo's reputation  
* not a passive protagonist

Very important:

Sara should **not** become “the girl whose entire personality is loving Gojo.”

She has her own life, studies, interests, opinions and decisions.

She can meet the strongest sorcerer alive and basically think:

> “Wow, he's hot.”

while simultaneously saying:

> “I actually need to get to the library.”

And then unexpectedly:

> **“Btw, can I get your number?”**

That boldness is part of her charm.

# Gojo \+ Sara’s initial Dynamic

The first meeting is intentionally asymmetric.

### **Gojo's perspective:**

He notices something unusual about Sara before he consciously understands what it is.

His cursed energy reacts to her.

His blindfold lifts.

He looks at her.

His first real question is:

> **Who are you?**

### **Sara's perspective:**

She mostly notices:

> He's extremely attractive / cool.

She doesn't initially know who he is or understand why he's interested.

So the opening dynamic is:

> **Gojo notices the mystery.**  
> **Sara notices the man.**

This is important.

# Sara’s “Warmth” Line

In the original chat, Sara tells Gojo something along the lines of:

> she finds him reliable somehow  
> he gives off warmth  
> she doesn't know why  
> she normally hasn't trusted that feeling from a stranger

This is a key emotional line.

However, in the revised novel version, we decided **not to make it sound like Sara has deeply understood Gojo after five minutes.**

Instead, it should feel like the statement **slips out of her unexpectedly**, even surprising Sara herself.

For example:

> “I don't know why, but...”

> She hesitated.

> “You seem reliable.”

> Gojo blinked.

> “Reliable?”

> “Yeah. You give off this... warmth. It's strange. I trust you, and I don't even know you.”

This makes the line emotionally powerful without making the relationship advance unrealistically fast.

# Important Romance Motifs

## **1\. Warmth**

Sara experiences Gojo as warm/safe.

Gojo is affected because people normally experience him as powerful/terrifying/untouchable.

Eventually Gojo becomes one of Sara's emotional anchors.

## **2\. Blindfold**

The blindfold is the strongest recurring symbol.

Initially:

> Sara is curious about what is beneath it.

Later:

> The blindfold represents Gojo's emotional armor and the “Strongest” persona.

Sara wants to see his eyes.

Metaphorically, she wants to see **Satoru behind the Strongest**.

Meanwhile Gojo gradually wants to understand what is behind Sara's unexplained experiences.

The title **An Attractive Guy Behind the Blindfold** therefore eventually has a deeper meaning.

## **3\. Food**

Recurring relationship motifs:

* mochi  
* desserts  
* pancakes  
* restaurants  
* spontaneous food stops

Food scenes should be used to create ordinary, warm relationship moments.

Not every chapter needs plot progression.

Some chapters can simply make readers fall in love with the characters.

# Dream Mystery

Sara starts having dreams about Gojo.

Initially the dreams can seem romantic/beautiful.

One important dream contains:

* sunset  
* autumn  
* traditional Japanese clothing  
* Sara wearing a cream/yellow/brown kimono  
* Gojo wearing a black/red yukata  
* bustling market/shop streets  
* autumn leaves  
* fireworks

Sara eventually tells Gojo about this dream.

Gojo reacts strangely because some details correspond to things he knows.

In the original interaction, Gojo jokes about the dream while privately being affected by the similarities.

---

# Dream Escalation

The dreams gradually stop being simply romantic.

Sara begins experiencing:

* places she shouldn't know  
* memories that don't feel like hers  
* strange commands  
* voices  
* increasingly vivid experiences

One important dream sequence involves Sara trying to remove Gojo's blindfold.

The dream contains emotional/sad elements, and a voice essentially tells her:

> **continue**

The dream eventually becomes disturbing enough that Sara wakes up crying in reality.

This is where readers should begin realizing:

> These dreams may not actually be dreams.

The exact identity/source of the voice should remain mysterious for a long time.

Possible possibilities early in the story:

* cursed object  
* Sara's hidden ability  
* her childhood connection  
* Sukuna  
* something connected to the Korean monastery  
* something else

Do not reveal the answer too early.

---

# Sara’s Hidden Ability

Sara has an unexplained sensitivity to **concentrated/high-energy supernatural presences**.

She does NOT suddenly become a conventional jujutsu sorcerer.

She cannot simply see every curse or start fighting like a trained sorcerer.

Instead, she can:

* sense unusually strong energy  
* instinctively notice people like Gojo and Yuji  
* be drawn toward high-energy supernatural presences  
* eventually sense the cursed object

She doesn't understand that this is an ability.

Gojo gradually recognises the pattern.

This explains why:

* Gojo's cursed energy reacts to her  
* Sara naturally notices Gojo  
* Sara can connect with Yuji  
* Sara eventually finds the cursed object

---

# Gojo’s Background Investigation

At some point Gojo begins investigating Sara.

Initially:

> Her records are completely clean.

Nothing obviously supernatural appears.

Then he notices where she was born in Korea.

That location was near an old **cursed Buddhist monastery / supernatural site**.

The preferred version of this idea is:

> The monastery was a place where something had been **sealed**.

Not simply:

> “Sara was born next to a haunted monastery.”

Sara may have been exposed to something unusual when she was young, leaving her with her unexplained sensitivity.

Gojo gradually connects:

**Sara's birthplace → monastery → unusual sensitivity → dreams → cursed object**

He is investigating this while continuing his normal romantic/playful relationship with Sara.

This gives us two Gojo layers:

### **Public Gojo:**

Funny, arrogant, teasing, affectionate, ridiculous.

### **Private Gojo:**

Observant, suspicious, increasingly worried and protective.

# Engineering / Excavation Arc

Sara's actual PhD/research work eventually takes her to a site excavation.

This is important because the supernatural plot should arise naturally from **her real life**.

At the excavation she finds something that ordinary people were never supposed to find:

> **a cursed object**

This becomes the major turning point.

Before this:

> The supernatural mystery is happening around Sara.

After this:

> **The supernatural has physically entered Sara's life.**

---

# Cursed Object

We decided it should preferably **not simply be another Sukuna finger**.

Instead:

> A relic/fragment/object connected to Sukuna.

It contains residual cursed energy associated with Sukuna.

It is capable of resonating with Sukuna because Sukuna is inside Yuji.

The supernatural connection becomes:

**Sara ↔ cursed object ↔ Yuji/Sukuna**

Sara can sense the object because of her hidden sensitivity.

The object reacts to Sara.

Sukuna eventually becomes aware of her through the connection.

---

# Voices

After Sara encounters the object, the voices become stronger.

Initially:

* ambiguous  
* occasional  
* unclear

Eventually:

* commands  
* increasingly direct  
* frightening

Possible messages include:

> continue  
> remember  
> find it  
> remove the blindfold

The key mystery is:

> **Is Sara hearing something outside herself, or something inside herself?**

---

# Sukuna / Yuji Arc

Eventually Sara spends time with Yuji.

Because of the connection between the cursed object and Sukuna, Sukuna becomes aware of Sara.

At some point Sukuna awakens / exerts control and Sara becomes affected by the cursed object.

This becomes the major supernatural conflict.

---

# Climax

Gojo eventually has to help Sara.

But Sara must NOT be a helpless damsel whose entire role is waiting for Gojo to rescue her.

Her supernatural sensitivity becomes important.

She can understand/distinguish something about the cursed energy that helps with the resolution.

The intended emotional structure is:

> **Gojo fights to bring Sara back.**

> **Sara fights to find her way back herself.**

The resolution should therefore be something they accomplish together.

---

# Emotional Climax

The supernatural resolution should circle back to the central emotional theme.

At the beginning:

> Sara instinctively experiences warmth from Gojo.

During the story:

> Gojo becomes someone Sara feels safe with.

During the climax:

> Gojo becomes an anchor while Sara fights to remain herself.

The emotional conclusion is not simply:

> “Gojo defeated the curse.”

It is closer to:

> **Sara chose herself, and Gojo stayed with her while she did.**

### **Normal:**

**1,200–1,800 words**

### **Short:**

**700–1,000 words**

Used occasionally for:

* emotional beats  
* dreams  
* cliffhangers  
* transitions

### **Major:**

**1,800–2,500 words**

For:

* major supernatural revelations  
* possession  
* confrontation

The important rule:

> **A chapter ends when the scene has accomplished what it needs to accomplish, not when it reaches an arbitrary word count.**

Consistency should be in the *reading experience*, not exact word count.

Dual POV is preferred, primarily:

* Gojo POV  
* Sara POV

But it should NOT alternate mechanically every chapter.

Gojo's internal thoughts are especially valuable because of the contrast between:

### **Outward Gojo:**

> “Haha, I'm handsome and I'm the strongest.”

### **Inner Gojo:**

> “Why does she make me feel this way?”

The original Tipsy material contains a lot of this contrast and should be adapted into proper prose.

# Important Dialogue Refinement Instruction

The original AI chat sometimes makes Gojo and Sara jump too quickly into emotionally intimate or overly polished dialogue.

For the novel:

**Keep the important ideas/lines, but make the conversation sound like two strangers actually meeting.**

Especially Sara's warmth statement.

It should feel like she surprised herself by saying it rather than like she has already emotionally analyzed Gojo.

Example:

> “I don't know why, but...”

> She hesitated.

> “You seem reliable.”

> Gojo blinked.

> “Reliable?”

> “Yeah. You give off this... warmth. It's strange. I trust you, and I don't even know you.”

This is preferred because it preserves the key idea without making the relationship move too quickly.

# Timeline, Setting, and Naming Conventions

## Canon timeline placement

* The main story of *Jujutsu Kaisen* Season 1 begins in **spring/summer 2018**.
* This fanfic is set in **2018, during the Season 1 timeframe**, beginning in **September 2018**, when Sara starts her PhD at the University of Tokyo.
  * Yuji, Megumi, and Nobara have already met, trained together, and gone through early missions.
  * The **Kyoto Goodwill Event has NOT happened yet** at the start of the story. It may occur later in the story’s timeline or be delayed/modified to fit the AU. No specific date is fixed yet.
  * Gojo is already established as their teacher and the "strongest" sorcerer.
  * The world still feels mostly normal on the surface, which supports the romance-first tone.

**Canon divergence:** The **Shibuya Incident is delayed to 2021** in this fanfic. Kenjaku and the Shibuya plot are not referenced in the story's early parts, and no in-text dates should contradict this.

**Yuji and Sara:** They became online friends while Yuji was still at his previous school, before he enrolled at Jujutsu High. Yuji never told her about curses or sorcerers until Chapter 2. Yuji and Sara met online roughly a year before the story begins, while he was still in middle school / early high school in Sendai.

You do not need to state the year explicitly in every chapter, but keep technology and references consistent with **2018 Japan** (smartphones, messaging apps, social media, food blogs, viral videos, arcade games, convenience stores, observatories, shrines). No futuristic tech, and no pandemic-related element.

## How characters address Gojo in canon

In *Jujutsu Kaisen*:

* Students (Yuji, Megumi, Nobara, etc.) call him **“Gojo-sensei.”**  
* Other adult sorcerers and peers usually call him **“Gojo.”**  
* Only people who have known him since youth or are very close (e.g., Geto, Shoko in flashbacks) call him **“Satoru.”**

Civilians who know him as “Yuji’s teacher” might use **“Gojo-san”** or simply **“Gojo.”** They would not normally call him “Satoru” unless they become extremely close.

## Naming convention in this fanfic

To reinforce the theme “Everyone sees Gojo, the Strongest; Sara slowly sees Satoru,” use the following rule:

* In Sara’s POV chapters (and any third-person narration closely following her):  
  * Refer to him as **“Gojo”** (or “Gojo-san” in dialogue if she’s being polite).  
  * Do **not** use “Satoru” in her internal narration until much later in the story, when their relationship is deeply intimate.  
* In Gojo’s POV chapters (and third-person narration closely following his thoughts):  
  * Refer to him as **“Satoru”** in internal narration and when focusing on his personal feelings.  
  * Use **“Gojo”** when describing how others see him or when he’s performing his “strongest” persona.  
* In dialogue:  
  * Yuji, Megumi, Nobara: **“Gojo-sensei.”**\[[reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/JuJutsuKaisen/comments/miubv8/how_megumi_refers_to_gojo/)\]  
  * Sara: **“Gojo”** or **“Gojo-san”** initially; she may shift to just “Gojo” as they get closer.  
  * Save **“Satoru”** in spoken dialogue for a much later stage of their relationship, when both are clearly emotionally committed.
